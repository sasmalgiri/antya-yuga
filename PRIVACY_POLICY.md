# Privacy Policy — Anta Yuga

**Effective date:** 1 July 2026
**Last updated:** 1 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game **Anta Yuga** ("the App",
"we", "us") handles information when you install and use it.

By using Anta Yuga you agree to the practices described below. If you do
not agree, please uninstall the App.

---

## 1. Who runs the App

Anta Yuga is published by **EcoSanskriti Innovation** ("we").
Contact: sasmalgiri@gmail.com

---

## 2. What information the App collects

### 2.1 Information you choose to provide

- **None.** Anta Yuga does not ask for your name, email, phone number,
  age, location, photos, contacts, microphone, or camera at any point.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

- **Game progress, settings, and bazaar wallet** are stored ONLY on your
  device (via Apple's standard `UserDefaults` mechanism). This data
  never leaves your phone or iPad.
- **No analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or tracking framework is present
  in the App.** We do not see how often you play, which features you
  use, or what your score is.
- **No crash reporting service** beyond Apple's standard built-in
  diagnostics (which you control in Settings → Privacy & Security →
  Analytics & Improvements). If you have those toggles off, no crash
  data leaves your device.

### 2.3 In-App Purchases

Anta Yuga sells three consumable in-app point packs (Starter $0.99,
Hero $2.99, Maharaja $4.99) using Apple's StoreKit framework.

When you make a purchase:

- The transaction is processed entirely by **Apple**. Anta Yuga does NOT
  see your Apple ID, payment method, or billing address.
- We receive only an anonymous receipt token verifying that you have
  paid for a specific product. We use that token only to credit the
  Bazaar Points you bought to your local wallet.
- Apple's own privacy policy and terms apply to the transaction:
  https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

---

## 3. What information we share

We share NO personal information with anyone, because we collect none.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share data with advertisers, data
brokers, or third-party services.

---

## 4. How data is stored and protected

- Game data is stored locally in `UserDefaults`, which is sandboxed by
  iOS to be readable only by Anta Yuga on your device.
- We do not maintain a server. There is nothing to breach on our side
  because the App never transmits player data to us.
- Deleting the App from your device permanently deletes all game data
  associated with it.

---

## 5. Children's privacy

Anta Yuga is rated 9+ in the App Store. We do not knowingly collect
information from children under 13 because the App does not collect
identifying information from anyone of any age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us
information they should not have, contact us — though we expect to have
nothing of theirs.

---

## 6. Your rights

Because we do not have any personal data about you, there is nothing to
request, correct, port, or delete from our side.

You can permanently delete all on-device game data by:

1. Uninstalling Anta Yuga from your device, OR
2. iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Anta Yuga → Delete App.

For purchase history and refunds, contact Apple via
https://reportaproblem.apple.com — Apple, not us, is the merchant of
record for App Store purchases.

## 7. Region-specific notices

### European Union / United Kingdom (GDPR / UK-GDPR)

We do not collect or process personal data of EU or UK residents. No
data is transferred outside your device. Where the regulation refers to
a "data controller", the answer is: we have no data to control.

### California (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information of California
residents. We do not engage in "cross-context behavioural advertising".

### India (DPDP Act 2023)

We do not process digital personal data of Indian residents. No data
fiduciary or processor relationship exists.
