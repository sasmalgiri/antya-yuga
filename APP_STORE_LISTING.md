## Description (4000 char max)

```
Anta Yuga is a strategic tower-defence game inspired by Indian itihāsa —
the Ramayana, the Mahabharata, and the Puranas. Choose a dynasty. March
through three Yugas. Wield thirty divine astras against the demon horde.
At Wave 14, Kali Yuga himself arrives — and only the Sudarshana Chakra
stands between dharma and the End.

NINE DYNASTIES, NINE PLAYSTYLES
Raghuvansh, Maurya, Gupta, Pratihara, Rashtrakuta, Pal, Chola, Sen, Ahom.
Each lineage carries a unique combat or economy signature — heavier
projectiles, faster fire rate, deeper resource regeneration, or expanded
aura reach.

THIRTY DIVINE ASTRAS ACROSS THREE YUGAS
Begin with humble Dart Astras in Satya Yug. Unlock heal auras in Treta.
Unlock shield auras in Dvapara. Then forge the apex T4 ultimates —
Sudarshana, Brahmasira, Bhambhrastra, Pashupatastra — and combine three of
them to summon the दुर्गा-STRIKE, the Trimurti combination attack that
doubles every contributor's damage.

MYTHOLOGICAL ENEMIES
Pishachas and Rakshasas swarm the spiral path under bosses Mahishasura,
Ravana, Indrajit, Vritra, Bhasmasura, Putana — and at last the empowered
Kali Yuga, with 600,000 HP, 500 HP/s regeneration, and minion summons.

FIVE-RESOURCE ECONOMY
Gold, Metal, Tech, Jotisha, Veda. Build resource buildings during waves.
Socket Chuni, Panna, Nila and Raktamukhi stones into your towers for
timed amplification, or spend a stone on the Wall to refresh your defence
mid-fight.

THE SUDARSHANA CHAKRA
Feed the central relic gold. At 100% charge the chakra ignites at the
Kali wave and burns the dark age at 1,650 damage per second. Every divine
tower you have built amplifies the burn.

THE PARIJATA BRIKSHA
A wish-tree that compounds every divine kill — your late-game endgame
production scales with the faith you have planted.

BAZAAR
Spend Bazaar points on instant items, run-long buffs, and permanent
perks that carry across every future run. Earn Bazaar points by clearing
waves, or pick up an optional point pack from the in-app store.

NO TRACKING. EVER.
No analytics SDK. No advertising SDK. No accounts. No login. No data
leaves your device. Your run is yours alone — UserDefaults and the
StoreKit receipt are the only things we ever read.

INSPIRED WITH REVERENCE
Names of deities, asuras, astras and ages are drawn from public-domain
itihāsa and used with respect for the cultural and religious traditions
from which they originate. Nothing in this game is intended to make
light of any community's beliefs.

Free to download. Optional Bazaar point packs available. Designed for
iPhone, iPad, and macOS. Save dharma. End Kali.
```
*(~2 850 / 4 000)*
