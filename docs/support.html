Frequently asked questions

Why did my Bazaar points reset after a game?

Points earned in-run (from enemy kills and wave clears) are by design scoped to that match — they're part of the in-game economy. Permanent perks bought with them carry over between runs.

Does Anta Yuga have in-app purchases?

No. Anta Yuga is free to play with no in-app purchases. All Bazaar points are earned through gameplay — enemy kills and wave clears.

How do I delete my data?

Anta Yuga stores all data locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults. Deleting the app removes all saved progress, wallet balance, and unlocked perks. We do not store any data on our servers.
