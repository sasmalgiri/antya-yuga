Frequently asked questions

Why did my Bazaar points reset after a game?

Points earned in-run (from enemy kills and wave clears) are by design refunded only for that match — they're part of the in-game economy. Points you purchase from the App Store ("Get Points") go into your saved wallet and carry over between runs.

I purchased points but they didn't appear.

Open the Bazaar and tap Restore in the "Get Points" section. If the points still don't appear within a few minutes, contact us with your Apple order number — Apple sometimes delays receipt delivery.

Can I get a refund for in-app purchases?

All in-app purchases are handled by Apple. Refund requests are managed through Apple's process: reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Astra Yug stores all data locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults. Deleting the app removes all saved progress, wallet balance, and unlocked perks. We do not store any data on our servers.
