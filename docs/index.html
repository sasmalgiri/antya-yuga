Defend the cosmos with divine astras.

A strategic tower-defence game inspired by Indian itihāsa. Choose your dynasty, unfold three yugas of progression, wield 24 named astras, and turn back demon hordes led by Ravana, Mahishasura, and Indrajit.

What's inside

4 dynasties

Raghuvansh, Sen, Pal and Maurya — each with a distinct combat and economy bonus.

24 divine astras

From the humble Dart to the apex Pashupatastra, evolved across three yugas.

14 mythological foes

Demon swarms, resource bearers, and boss tiers like Ravana with damage immunities.

5-resource economy

Gold, Metal, Tech, Jotisha and Veda — gathered by buildings during waves.

Power stones

Chuni, Panna, Nila and Raktamukhi sockets to deepen tower builds.

Bazaar & perks

Spend Bazaar points on in-run boosts, run-long buffs and permanent unlocks.

Inspired with reverence

Anta Yuga draws on the rich storytelling tradition of Indian itihāsa — the Ramayana, Mahabharata and Puranas. Names and references are used with respect for the source material. Nothing in this game is intended to make light of any community's beliefs or cultural heritage.
