Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how the Anta Yuga mobile application ("Anta Yuga", "the app", "we", "our") handles information when you use it on iOS or iPadOS.

1. Information we do not collect

Anta Yuga is designed to work without collecting personal information. Specifically, the app does not:

  • Require account creation, login, or any sign-up.
  • Collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, or contacts.
  • Track you across other apps or websites; we do not use the IDFA or any tracking framework.
  • Use third-party analytics, advertising, or attribution SDKs.
  • Transmit gameplay data to any server we control.

2. Information stored only on your device

The app uses Apple's UserDefaults system to save your progress on your device. This includes:

  • Your Bazaar point wallet balance (points purchased through Apple's in-app purchase system).
  • The set of permanent perks you have unlocked.
  • Audio mute preference.

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

3. In-app purchases

Anta Yuga offers consumable in-app purchases (point packs) processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. Children

The app is rated suitable for ages 9+ and does not knowingly collect information from children. Because the app does not collect any personal information from anyone, there is nothing to provide on request under children's privacy laws (such as COPPA or the UK Children's Code).

5. Required Reasons APIs

In compliance with Apple's required-reasons API rules, our PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest declares the following:

  • UserDefaults — used only to read and write information accessible to this app itself (reason code CA92.1).

6. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy we will revise the "Last updated" date above and publish the new version at the same URL.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to sasmalgiri@gmail.com.
